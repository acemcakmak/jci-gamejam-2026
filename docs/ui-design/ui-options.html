KOMUR UI OPTIONS

Mockups built from the real sprites: Sprout Lands UI, Complete UI Essential, Cainos, Pet Cats, Monsters and the PixelBattleText font. Pick one option per section and I'll implement those in Unity.

1 · Panel style (status panel)

The panel behind wave and villager counts, used for every panel (status, story, end screen, banner). Every option is 9-sliced, so text always fits inside the border.

2 · Minimap

The map is drawn from the town's tiles (grass, roads, square, raised areas, walls), not a camera snapshot, so it's always readable. Icons: Kömür = cat, villager = cream dot, cured = pink dot, sick = pulsing green "+" (turns red as time runs out), rat = red dot, apothecary = house, open rat hole = dark ring.

3 · Title screen

The game starts zoomed in on the square with Kömür sitting, then zooms out when you press Play.

4 · Wave banner

Short announcements: "WAVE 2/5", "A NEW RAT HOLE APPEARED!", "THE PLAGUE SLIME IS HERE!"

5 · Battle texts (PixelBattleText)

Rendered on a low-resolution overlay so they come out big and crisp, and spawned right above the character involved. These replace "Thanks!" and the other plain world texts.

6 · Character scale

Kömür and the rats are too small next to Cainos props. Compare sizes against a villager and a crate. The game camera shows about 10 tiles of height.
