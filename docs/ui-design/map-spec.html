Quarters

NW · Chapel Hill (upper floor)A raised hill with a southern bulge and an eastern spur. The ruined church has two rows of pillars, an altar and a statue, with graves behind it. Main stairs on the bulge, side stairs to the east. Rat hole 4 is up on the hill.
NE · MarketA stone market plaza with stall rows and a well. A merchant terrace (upper floor) with stairs, plus a shop, a warehouse and a gatehouse by the north road. Rat hole 2 is on the east edge.
SW · FarmsteadA barn, a cottage and a walled garden, rows of flowers as crops, and a farm lane south off the west road. Rat hole 1 is on the west edge.
SE · Sunken graveyardA walled pit with rows of graves, a mausoleum and broken rune pillars. You go down a west ramp from the graveyard path or a north ramp towards the market. Rat hole 3 is inside the pit.
Centre · Town squareA stone plaza with steps out to every road. It has the well, a statue, pillars on the corners and the apothecary. Kömür starts here.
ReadabilityTrees stay on the town edges and between quarters, never over roads, plazas or just south of cliffs. Inside the quarters there are only low bushes, rocks and flowers.
